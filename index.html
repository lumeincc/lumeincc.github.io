02 / Problems

We start from the business problem, not the tech stack.

ProblemSolution

  • Problem: Leads get lost in chats and spreadsheets.

    →

    Solution: A CRM or a Telegram bot: every lead is logged, assigned and tracked to a deal.

  • Problem: The website brings no clients.

    →

    Solution: A new site built for one goal: a request or a purchase. Fast, clear, with analytics.

  • Problem: Staff lose hours on manual routine.

    →

    Solution: Automation: reports, exports and notifications run without people.

  • Problem: Customers can't order from their phone.

    →

    Solution: A mobile app or a Telegram Mini App with ordering and payment.

  • Problem: Your systems don't talk to each other.

    →

    Solution: API integration: data moves between services with no copy-paste.

  • Problem: You have a product idea but no team.

    →

    Solution: We build the first version, launch it and keep developing it.

  1. Brief

    We break down the task and lock the requirements. Hard questions come before the start, not after.

    ResultScope, timeline, price

  2. Build

    ResultA working version at every stage
